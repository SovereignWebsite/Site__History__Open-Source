## The facts

### July 2000: Fowler picked out the same point Raymond had made in "Linus's Law": in open source, debugging can be done by many people in parallel.

> A particular feature of open source development is that debugging is highly parallelizable.

- Source (primary, own-post): [The New Methodology (Original)](https://martinfowler.com/articles/newMethodologyOriginal.html), martinfowler.com, published 21 July 2000. Read 27 November 2026.
- Connects: Martin Fowler, Eric S. Raymond, The New Methodology, The Cathedral and the Bazaar
- Fact id: `fowler-open-source-debugging-2000`

### July 2000: In the original July 2000 version of "The New Methodology", Martin Fowler listed open source alongside the new lightweight methods, saying much of its way of working applied to closed source projects too.

> However there is a definite way of doing things in the open source community, and much of their approach is as applicable to closed source projects as it is to open source.

- Source (primary, own-post): [The New Methodology (Original)](https://martinfowler.com/articles/newMethodologyOriginal.html), martinfowler.com, published 21 July 2000. Read 27 November 2026.
- Note: Fowler republished the original text himself; the current version of the article no longer has the section.
- Connects: Martin Fowler, The New Methodology
- Fact id: `fowler-open-source-new-methodology-2000`
