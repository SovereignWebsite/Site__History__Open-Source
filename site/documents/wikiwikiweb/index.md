## The facts

### 1995: Cunningham confirmed in correspondence with a dictionary editor that he named the technology WikiWikiWeb in 1995, after the Hawaiian word for quick.

> I chose to call the technology WikiWikiWeb.

- Source (primary, own-post): [Correspondence on the Etymology of Wiki](http://c2.com/doc/etymology.html), Cunningham & Cunningham, Inc. (c2.com), published November 2003. Read 27 November 2026.
- Note: In the same reply, asked "In 1995?", he answers "Yes."
- Connects: Ward Cunningham, WikiWikiWeb
- Fact id: `cunningham-wiki-name-1995`

### 25 March 1995: Ward Cunningham started the first wiki, the WikiWikiWeb, on 25 March 1995 as a supplement to the Portland Pattern Repository.

> This is the first ever wiki site, founded as an automated supplement to the PortlandPatternRepository.

- Source (primary, archive): [WikiHistory (page of the original wiki, WikiWikiWeb)](https://c2.com/wiki/remodel/pages/WikiHistory), Cunningham & Cunningham, Inc. (c2.com). Read 27 November 2026.
- Note: From the WikiHistory page of the original wiki, read from the site's page data (JSON). The page opens "This began on March 25, 1995." and continues in the first person ("I created the site"); it is a wiki page and so could have been edited by others.
- Connects: Ward Cunningham, WikiWikiWeb
- Fact id: `cunningham-first-wiki-1995`
