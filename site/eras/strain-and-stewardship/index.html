Strain and stewardship

2018 to 2026.

Two of the largest open source companies were bought, GitHub by Microsoft and Red Hat by IBM12. Vendors squeezed by cloud providers left open source licences and some returned345. Supply-chain attacks and vulnerabilities67 brought foundations and regulators in89, and the meaning of "open source" for AI is contested1011.

19 January 2021 Strain and stewardship

On 19 January 2021 the Open Source Initiative stated that the SSPL is not an open source licence, calling such licences "fauxpen" source.

Fauxpen source licenses allow a user to view the source code but do not allow other highly important rights protected by the Open Source Definition, such as the right to make use of the program for any field of endeavor.

Primary source: press-release The SSPL is Not an Open Source License, Open Source Initiative, published 19 January 2021. Read 27 November 2026.

Connects: Open Source Initiative, Elastic, MongoDB, Inc., Server Side Public License, The Open Source Definition

28 October 2024 Strain and stewardship

The Open Source AI Definition requires detailed information about the training data, enough for a skilled person to build a substantially equivalent system, but not the training data itself.

Data Information: Sufficiently detailed information about the data used to train the system so that a skilled person can build a substantially equivalent system.

Primary source: document The Open Source AI Definition 1.0, Open Source Initiative, published 28 October 2024. Read 27 November 2026.

That the data itself is not required is the reading of the definition's text and is the point its critics attack; see the Software Freedom Conservancy and FSF facts.

Connects: Open Source Initiative, The Open Source AI Definition 1.0

10 December 2024 Strain and stewardship

The European Union's Cyber Resilience Act entered into force on 10 December 2024, with its main obligations applying from 11 December 2027.

The CRA entered into force on 10 December 2024. The main obligations introduced by the Act will apply from 11 December 2027, with reporting obligations to apply as of 11 September 2026.

Primary source: document Cyber Resilience Act, European Commission. Read 27 November 2026.

Connects: European Union, Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)

Link to this fact

10 December 2024 Strain and stewardship

The Commission says only open source software supplied in the course of a commercial activity falls within the Act.

only free and open-source software that is made available on the market, and therefore supplied for distribution or use in the course of a commercial activity, falls in scope of the Cyber Resilience Act.

Primary source: document The Cyber Resilience Act and open source, European Commission. Read 27 November 2026.

Connects: European Union, Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)

Link to this fact

10 December 2024 Strain and stewardship

The Act creates a new legal category, the open source software steward, for bodies that sustain open source products used commercially, under a lighter regime than manufacturers.

the novel legal category of open-source software stewards is introduced. These are legal persons who provide support on a sustained basis for the development of such products which are intended for commercial activities, and who play a main role in ensuring their viability, and are subject to a light-touch and tailor-made regulatory regime.

Primary source: document The Cyber Resilience Act and open source, European Commission. Read 27 November 2026.

From the European Commission's explanatory page. The text of the Regulation on EUR-Lex returned a 202 challenge page and could not be read with the fetch tool.

Connects: European Union, Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)
