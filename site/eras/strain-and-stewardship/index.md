# Strain and stewardship

2018 to 2026.

Two of the largest open source companies were bought, GitHub by Microsoft and Red Hat by IBM [1] [2]. Vendors squeezed by cloud providers left open source licences and some returned [3] [4] [5]. Supply-chain attacks and vulnerabilities [6] [7] brought foundations and regulators in [8] [9], and the meaning of "open source" for AI is contested [10] [11].
