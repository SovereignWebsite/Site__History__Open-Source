## The facts

### 11 August 1998: The same memorandum concluded that open source was credible in the long term and could not be fought with fear, uncertainty and doubt.

> OSS is long-term credible ... FUD tactics can not be used to combat it.

- Source (primary, document): [Halloween Document I (Microsoft memorandum "Open Source Software: A (New?) Development Methodology", annotated)](http://catb.org/~esr/halloween/halloween1.html), Eric S. Raymond (catb.org), published 1 November 1998. Read 27 November 2026.
- Note: Quoted from Raymond's list of "key quotes", where the ellipsis is his; the full sentence is further down in the memorandum.
- Connects: Vinod Valloppillil, Microsoft, Halloween Document I, Leak of the Halloween Documents
- Fact id: `halloween-memo-fud-1998`

### 11 August 1998: An internal Microsoft memorandum of August 1998 judged open source software to be a direct, short-term revenue and platform threat to the company.

> OSS poses a direct, short-term revenue and platform threat to Microsoft, particularly in server space.

- Source (primary, document): [Halloween Document I (Microsoft memorandum "Open Source Software: A (New?) Development Methodology", annotated)](http://catb.org/~esr/halloween/halloween1.html), Eric S. Raymond (catb.org), published 1 November 1998. Read 27 November 2026.
- Note: The memorandum (author Vinod Valloppillil, dated "Aug 11, 1998 -- v1.00" in the text) was leaked to Eric Raymond, who published it with his annotations on 1 November 1998. It is read here in Raymond's annotated copy; Microsoft has not published the original.
- Connects: Vinod Valloppillil, Eric S. Raymond, Microsoft, Halloween Document I, Leak of the Halloween Documents
- Fact id: `halloween-memo-threat-1998`

### November 1998: According to Raymond, Microsoft admitted that the leaked memorandum was authentic within days of its publication.

> Pressed, Microsoft admitted the authenticity of Halloween I later on Monday

- Source (primary, own-post): [Halloween Document III: Microsoft's Reaction to the "Halloween Memorandum"](http://catb.org/~esr/halloween/halloween3.html), Eric S. Raymond (catb.org), published 5 November 1998. Read 27 November 2026.
- Note: Usually paraphrased. This is Raymond's statement, written on 5 November 1998, not Microsoft's own words. The same page reproduces a response attributed to Microsoft Netherlands. No Microsoft press statement from 1998 could be fetched.
- Connects: Eric S. Raymond, Microsoft, Leak of the Halloween Documents
- Fact id: `halloween-authenticity-1998`
