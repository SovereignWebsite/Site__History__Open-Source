# Events

Every event here is linked from at least one sourced fact.

- ["Get the Facts" campaign](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/get-the-facts-campaign/) (1 fact, 2004): Microsoft advertising campaign comparing Windows with Linux.
- [Announcement of the GNU Project](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/gnu-announcement-1983/) (1 fact, 1983): Stallman's Usenet post announcing a free Unix-compatible system.
- [Craig Mundie's speech at NYU Stern](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/mundie-nyu-speech-2001/) (2 facts, 2001): Speech on "The Commercial Software Model" attacking the GPL and introducing Shared Source.
- [First devopsdays](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/devopsdays-2009/) (1 fact, 2009): Conference held in Ghent, Belgium, in 2009.
- [Freeware Summit / Open Source Summit](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/open-source-summit-1998/) (2 facts, 1998): Summit of leading developers in Palo Alto organised by Tim O'Reilly.
- [Heartbleed](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/heartbleed-2014/) (2 facts, 2014): Vulnerability in the OpenSSL library disclosed in April 2014.
- [IBM's acquisition of Red Hat](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/red-hat-acquisition/) (2 facts, 2018 to 2019): Announced on 28 October 2018 and closed on 9 July 2019, at about $34 billion.
- [Leak of the Halloween Documents](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/halloween-documents-1998/) (3 facts, 1998): Publication by Eric Raymond of leaked Microsoft memoranda on open source.
- [Log4Shell](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/log4shell-2021/) (1 fact, 2021): Critical vulnerability in Apache Log4j.
- [Microsoft cloud event, San Francisco](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/microsoft-cloud-event-2014/) (4 facts, 2014 to 2015): Press event at which Nadella showed the "Microsoft loves Linux" slide.
- [Microsoft's acquisition of GitHub](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/github-acquisition-2018/) (2 facts, 2018): Announced on 4 June 2018 for $7.5 billion and completed on 26 October 2018.
- [Netscape source code announcement](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/netscape-announcement-1998/) (2 facts, 1998): Netscape's announcement that it would publish the Communicator source.
- [SCO v. IBM](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/sco-v-ibm/) (2 facts, 2003 to 2021): Lawsuit begun in 2003 and dismissed after a settlement in 2021.
- [Steve Ballmer's Chicago Sun-Times interview](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/ballmer-sun-times-interview-2001/) (3 facts, 2001): Interview in which Ballmer was reported to have called Linux a cancer.
- [The strategy session that adopted "open source"](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/open-source-strategy-session-1998/) (2 facts, 1998): Meeting in Silicon Valley in the first week of February 1998, dated 3 February by the OSI and 5 February by Peterson.
- [The xz backdoor](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/xz-backdoor-2024/) (1 fact, 2024): Backdoor in the xz compression library reported by Andres Freund.
- [Unix System Laboratories v. BSDI and the University of California](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/events/usl-v-bsdi/) (1 fact, 1994): Lawsuit over Unix code in BSD, settled in January 1994.
