# Freeware Summit / Open Source Summit

Summit of leading developers in Palo Alto organised by Tim O'Reilly [1] [2].

## The facts

### April 1998: The April 1998 meeting was announced as the "Freeware Summit" and within a week was being called the "Open Source Summit".

> Announced in advance as the first "Freeware Summit," by April 14 it was referred to as the first "Open Source Summit."

- Source (primary, own-post): [How I coined the term 'open source'](https://opensource.com/article/18/2/coining-term-open-source-software), Opensource.com (Red Hat), published 1 February 2018. Read 27 November 2026.
- Note: The OSI history page calls the same meeting the "April 1998 Free Software Summit", so three names are in use for it.
- Connects: Tim O'Reilly, Christine Peterson, Freeware Summit / Open Source Summit
- Fact id: `freeware-summit-renamed-1998`

### 7 April 1998: At a summit organised by Tim O'Reilly in Palo Alto on 7 April 1998, leading developers agreed that "open source" or "sourceware" best described their way of working.

> the developers agreed that commercial development of the software is part of the picture, and that the terms "open source" or "sourceware" best describe the development method they support.

- Source (primary, press-release): [Open Source Pioneers Meet in Historic Summit (O'Reilly press release, as reprinted in Linux Gazette issue 28)](https://linuxgazette.net/issue28/oreilly.html), O'Reilly & Associates, reprinted by Linux Gazette, published 15 April 1998. Read 27 November 2026.
- Note: The press release is read as reprinted in Linux Gazette issue 28, where it is dated April 15, 1998; O'Reilly's own copy returned 403 to this machine.
- Connects: Tim O'Reilly, O'Reilly & Associates, Freeware Summit / Open Source Summit
- Fact id: `open-source-summit-1998`
