Organisations

Every organisation here is linked from at least one sourced fact.

  • .NET Foundation 1 fact, 2014Foundation through which Microsoft said it would work with the community on open source .NET.
  • Apache Group and Apache Software Foundation 3 facts, 1995 to 2021The group behind the Apache web server (1995), incorporated as a foundation in 1999.
  • Bell Labs 1 fact, 1969Research laboratory where Unix was created in 1969.
  • Canonical 1 fact, 2016Maker of Ubuntu and Microsoft's partner for the Windows Subsystem for Linux.
  • Cloud Native Computing Foundation 2 facts, 2015 to 2016Foundation announced in 2015 that took on Kubernetes in 2016.
  • CodePlex Foundation 1 fact, 2009Open source foundation sponsored by Microsoft in 2009.
  • Debian Project 3 facts, 1993 to 1998Volunteer-run distribution founded in 1993, whose guidelines became the basis of the Open Source Definition.
  • Elastic 4 facts, 2021 to 2024Search company that left the Apache licence in 2021 and added the AGPL in 2024.
  • European Union 4 facts, 2024Legislator of the Cyber Resilience Act, in force since December 2024.
  • Foresight Institute 1 fact, 1998Think tank where Christine Peterson worked when she proposed "open source".
  • Free Software Foundation 9 facts, 1985 to 2024Foundation set up in October 1985 to support GNU; publisher of the GPL.
  • GitHub 11 facts, 2008 to 2026Code hosting service launched in 2008, owned by Microsoft since 2018.
  • GNU Project 5 facts, 1983 to 1996Project announced by Stallman in 1983 to build a complete free Unix-compatible system.
  • Google 5 facts, 2005 to 2020Company behind Summer of Code, Android and Kubernetes.
  • HashiCorp 2 facts, 2023Infrastructure software company that moved Terraform to the Business Source License in 2023.
  • IBM 7 facts, 1955 to 2021Computer company that shipped early software as source, committed $1 billion to Linux in 2000 and bought Red Hat in 2019.
  • Jenkins 3 facts, 2011 to 2024Open source continuous integration server, renamed from Hudson by a community vote in 2011.
  • Kubernetes 3 facts, 2014 to 2016Container orchestration project open sourced by Google in 2014.
  • Microsoft 34 facts, 1998 to 2025Software company that opposed open source around 2001 and later joined the Linux Foundation and bought GitHub.
  • Microsoft Open Technologies, Inc. 1 fact, 2012Microsoft subsidiary for open standards and open source, announced in 2012.
  • MIT Artificial Intelligence Lab 1 fact, 1971Laboratory whose software-sharing community Stallman joined in 1971.
  • MongoDB, Inc. 5 facts, 2018 to 2021Database company that created the Server Side Public License in 2018.
  • Mozilla 2 facts, 1998 to 2004Project created in 1998 from the Netscape source, later a foundation and the maker of Firefox.
  • Netscape Communications 4 facts, 1998Browser company whose January 1998 decision to publish its source prompted the "open source" label.
  • O'Reilly & Associates 1 fact, 1998Publisher that hosted the April 1998 summit.
  • Open Handset Alliance 2 facts, 2007 to 2008Alliance of companies that announced Android in 2007.
  • Open Invention Network 2 facts, 2018Patent non-aggression community for Linux, which Microsoft joined in 2018.
  • Open Source Initiative 19 facts, 1998 to 2025Organisation founded in February 1998 by Raymond and Perens; keeper of the Open Source Definition and, since 2024, the Open Source AI Definition.
  • Open Source Security Foundation 2 facts, 2014 to 2020Linux Foundation body formed in 2020 for the security of open source software.
  • OpenTofu 2 facts, 2023Open source alternative to Terraform under the Linux Foundation.
  • Red Hat 4 facts, 1999 to 2020Linux company that went public in 1999 and was acquired by IBM in 2019.
  • Redis 4 facts, 2024 to 2025Database company that left the BSD licence in 2024 and added the AGPL in 2025.
  • SHARE 2 facts, 1955User group of IBM computer installations, begun in 1955.
  • Software Freedom Conservancy 1 fact, 2024Non-profit that criticised the Open Source AI Definition in 2024.
  • Sun Microsystems 2 facts, 2000 to 2006Company that opened StarOffice in 2000 and committed Java to the GPL in 2006.
  • The Linux Foundation 6 facts, 2014 to 2024Non-profit that hosts Linux and many other projects, including the CNCF, OpenSSF, OpenTofu and Valkey.
  • The SCO Group 2 facts, 2003 to 2021Company that sued IBM over Unix and Linux in 2003 in a case dismissed in 2021.
  • Thoughtworks 1 fact, 2024Consultancy whose staff built CruiseControl, by Martin Fowler's account.
  • University of California, Berkeley (Computer Systems Research Group) 4 facts, 1977 to 1994Home of the Berkeley Software Distribution and its permissive licence.
  • Valkey 1 fact, 2024Linux Foundation project continuing Redis 7.2.4 under the BSD licence.
