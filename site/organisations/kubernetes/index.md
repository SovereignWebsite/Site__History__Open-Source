## The facts

### 2014: The Kubernetes project states that Google open sourced it in 2014.

> Google open sourced the Kubernetes project in 2014.

- Source (primary, document): [Kubernetes documentation: Overview](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/overview/), Kubernetes project. Read 27 November 2026.
- Connects: Google, Kubernetes
- Fact id: `kubernetes-google-2014`

### June 2014: Kubernetes was released to the public in June 2014.

> In June of 2014 when Kubernetes was released to the world, the list above was the sum total of its basic feature set.

- Source (primary, own-post): [The History of Kubernetes & the Community Behind It](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2018/07/20/the-history-of-kubernetes-the-community-behind-it/), Kubernetes blog, published 20 July 2018. Read 27 November 2026.
- Note: From a 2018 retrospective on the Kubernetes blog by Brendan Burns, one of its creators. Google's announcement of 10 June 2014 was fetched but its body text was not captured by the fetch tool; see kubernetes-google-2014 for the project's own statement that Google open sourced it.
- Connects: Kubernetes
- Fact id: `kubernetes-released-2014`

### 10 March 2016: On 10 March 2016 the Cloud Native Computing Foundation accepted Kubernetes as its first hosted project.

> today announced that Kubernetes has been accepted by the Technical Oversight Committee (TOC) and its IP will be transferred to the Foundation.

- Source (primary, press-release): [Cloud Native Computing Foundation accepts Kubernetes as first hosted project; Technical Oversight Committee elected](https://www.cncf.io/blog/2016/03/10/cloud-native-computing-foundation-accepts-kubernetes-as-first-hosted-project-technical-oversight-committee-elected/), Cloud Native Computing Foundation, published 10 March 2016. Read 27 November 2026.
- Connects: Cloud Native Computing Foundation, Kubernetes
- Fact id: `kubernetes-joins-cncf-2016`
