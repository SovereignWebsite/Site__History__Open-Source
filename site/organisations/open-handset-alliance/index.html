5 November 2007 Hostility and adoption

On 5 November 2007 Google and the Open Handset Alliance announced Android as an open platform for mobile devices.

A broad alliance of leading technology and wireless companies today joined forces to announce the development of Android, the first truly open and comprehensive platform for mobile devices.

Primary source: press-release Industry Leaders Announce Open Platform for Mobile Devices, Open Handset Alliance, published 5 November 2007. Read 27 November 2026.

On 21 October 2008 Google and its partners released the Android source code.
