7 April 1998 The label

At a summit organised by Tim O'Reilly in Palo Alto on 7 April 1998, leading developers agreed that "open source" or "sourceware" best described their way of working.

the developers agreed that commercial development of the software is part of the picture, and that the terms "open source" or "sourceware" best describe the development method they support.

Primary source: press-release Open Source Pioneers Meet in Historic Summit (O'Reilly press release, as reprinted in Linux Gazette issue 28), O'Reilly & Associates, reprinted by Linux Gazette, published 15 April 1998. Read 27 November 2026.

The press release is read as reprinted in Linux Gazette issue 28, where it is dated April 15, 1998; O'Reilly's own copy returned 403 to this machine.
