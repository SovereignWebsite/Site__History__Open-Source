February 1998 The label

Christine Peterson, then executive director of the Foresight Institute, says she came up with the term "open source software" in the first week of February 1998.

Between meetings that week, I was still focused on the need for a better name and came up with the term "open source software."

Primary source: own-post How I coined the term 'open source', Opensource.com (Red Hat), by Christine Peterson, published 1 February 2018. Read 27 November 2026.

Her account was written on 2 January 2006 and published on Opensource.com in February 2018.

Connects: Christine Peterson, Foresight Institute

3 February 1998 The label

The Open Source Initiative's own history says the "open source" label was created at a strategy session in Palo Alto on 3 February 1998, and credits Christine Peterson with suggesting it.

The “open source” label was created at a strategy session held on February 3rd, 1998 in Palo Alto, California, shortly after the announcement of the release of the Netscape source code.

Primary source: document History of the OSI, Open Source Initiative. Read 27 November 2026.

Disputed. The OSI page gives 3 February 1998; Peterson's first-hand narrative gives 5 February 1998 (see open-source-meeting-peterson-account). The same OSI page says the term was "originally suggested by Christine Peterson".

Connects: Christine Peterson, Open Source Initiative, The strategy session that adopted "open source"

5 February 1998 The label

In Peterson's account the term was adopted at a strategy meeting at VA Research on 5 February 1998, where Todd Anderson put it forward and Eric Raymond endorsed it.

Later that week, on February 5, 1998, a group was assembled at VA Research to brainstorm on strategy. [...] Todd advocated the "open source" model, and Eric endorsed this.

Primary source: own-post How I coined the term 'open source', Opensource.com (Red Hat), by Christine Peterson, published 1 February 2018. Read 27 November 2026.

Disputed. The date is contested between the sources. Peterson's narrative says 5 February 1998, and Raymond's call of 8 February 1998 also points to 5 February; the Open Source Initiative's history says 3 February 1998, and the editor's introduction to Peterson's own article calls 3 February the anniversary.

Connects: Christine Peterson, Eric S. Raymond, Todd Anderson, The strategy session that adopted "open source"

April 1998 The label

The April 1998 meeting was announced as the "Freeware Summit" and within a week was being called the "Open Source Summit".

Announced in advance as the first "Freeware Summit," by April 14 it was referred to as the first "Open Source Summit."

Primary source: own-post How I coined the term 'open source', Opensource.com (Red Hat), by Christine Peterson, published 1 February 2018. Read 27 November 2026.

The OSI history page calls the same meeting the "April 1998 Free Software Summit", so three names are in use for it.

Connects: Tim O'Reilly, Christine Peterson, Freeware Summit / Open Source Summit
