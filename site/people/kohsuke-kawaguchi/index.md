# Kohsuke Kawaguchi

Creator of the Hudson continuous integration server, which its community renamed Jenkins in 2011 [1] [2].

## Connect the dots

- [Jenkins](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/organisations/jenkins/) (2 shared facts)

## The facts

### 11 January 2011: The reason given for leaving the Hudson name was that Oracle held the trademark and could revoke the project's right to use it.

> this trademark ownership gives Oracle the ability to revoke the Hudson project’s right to call itself Hudson at any time

- Source (primary, own-post): [Hudson's future](https://www.jenkins.io/blog/2011/01/11/hudsons-future), Jenkins project blog, published 11 January 2011. Read 27 November 2026.
- Connects: Kohsuke Kawaguchi, Jenkins
- Fact id: `jenkins-trademark-reason-2011`

### 29 January 2011: In January 2011 the community behind the Hudson continuous integration server voted 214 to 14 to rename the project Jenkins.

> The final result of all eligible votes is as follows: 214 votes to rename, and 14 for the status quo.

- Source (primary, own-post): [Jenkins!](https://www.jenkins.io/blog/2011/01/29/jenkins), Jenkins project blog, published 29 January 2011. Read 27 November 2026.
- Connects: Kohsuke Kawaguchi, Jenkins
- Fact id: `jenkins-rename-2011`
