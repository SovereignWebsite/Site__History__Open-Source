How it evolved

Each thread follows one change through time, step by step. Every step is a fact with its source.

  • The label, 1998 15 steps, 1997 to 1999Ten weeks, from Netscape's announcement to the summit that settled on the words "open source", followed step by step from the participants' own accounts.
  • The hostility 19 steps, 1976 to 2021The case made against shared and open software by those who sold software, from 1976 to the lawsuits and campaigns of the 2000s, and how it ended.
  • Corporate adoption 21 steps, 1995 to 2019How companies went from tolerating open source to building their businesses on it, and then to buying its institutions.
  • Microsoft and Linux: from "a cancer" to "Microsoft loves Linux" 28 steps, 1998 to 2025One company's change of position, step by step, each step from a document: the leaked memo, the speeches and interviews of 2001, the first kernel contribution, the 2014 slide, and what followed.
  • Licences and relicensing 26 steps, 1985 to 2025The licences are the mechanism. This thread follows the two families, copyleft and permissive, the definitions built on them, and the recent moves away from both.
  • Security and sustainability 8 steps, 1997 to 2024The promise that many eyes make bugs shallow, and what happened when critical projects turned out to have very few eyes on them.
  • Where things stand 17 steps, 2018 to 2025The open questions of 2018 to 2026, each from the statements of the parties.
