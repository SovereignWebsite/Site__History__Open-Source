# How it evolved

Each thread follows one change through time, step by step. Every step is a fact with its source.

- [From shared software to free software to open source](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/threads/free-software-to-open-source/) (22 steps, 1955 to 2024): How a habit became a movement and then a label, and why the people who started the movement did not all accept the label.
- [The label, 1998](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/threads/the-label-1998/) (15 steps, 1997 to 1999): Ten weeks, from Netscape's announcement to the summit that settled on the words "open source", followed step by step from the participants' own accounts.
- [The hostility](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/threads/the-hostility/) (19 steps, 1976 to 2021): The case made against shared and open software by those who sold software, from 1976 to the lawsuits and campaigns of the 2000s, and how it ended.
- [Corporate adoption](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/threads/corporate-adoption/) (21 steps, 1995 to 2019): How companies went from tolerating open source to building their businesses on it, and then to buying its institutions.
- [Microsoft and Linux: from "a cancer" to "Microsoft loves Linux"](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/threads/microsoft-and-linux/) (28 steps, 1998 to 2025): One company's change of position, step by step, each step from a document: the leaked memo, the speeches and interviews of 2001, the first kernel contribution, the 2014 slide, and what followed.
- [Licences and relicensing](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/threads/licences-and-relicensing/) (26 steps, 1985 to 2025): The licences are the mechanism. This thread follows the two families, copyleft and permissive, the definitions built on them, and the recent moves away from both.
- [Security and sustainability](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/threads/security-and-sustainability/) (8 steps, 1997 to 2024): The promise that many eyes make bugs shallow, and what happened when critical projects turned out to have very few eyes on them.
- [Where things stand](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/threads/state-now/) (17 steps, 2018 to 2025): The open questions of 2018 to 2026, each from the statements of the parties.
- [Where open source meets Agile](https://open-source.history.sovereignweb.site/threads/bridge-agile/) (15 steps, 1995 to 2024): The two histories share people, tools and one habit: put working software in front of others early and often. Each step here is a point where they touch.
